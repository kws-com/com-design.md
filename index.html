05 / COMPOSITION, SCROLL & LEGIBILITY

Design Conventions

Master grid main: 1 stretch row + 1 stretch column with 136px inset for static formats and responsive web framing with 1px gutters.

01 / PALETTE & SURFACES

Black (#000000), white (#FFFFFF), greys (#424242 / #EBE7E0), and the red accent family. Web containers use --blur-bg + blur(--blur-m).

02 / SHAPE CONTRAST

Passive containers and frames are strictly 0px sharp. Interactive buttons and directional controls are pill-rounded (999px) so they look clickable at first glance.

03 / WEB SCROLL MODES

Locked section-snap scroll is reserved for sales landing pages. Content-heavy sites (docs, articles) and all screens ≤ 960px always use unlocked natural scroll.

04 / FOOTER LEGIBILITY

The final section of a web page applies a dark bottom curtain over the shader so team cards, client logos (in --color-grey-l), and legal details remain crisp.

Strict Anti-Patterns (LLM Guardrails)

  • No rounded passive containers: Never apply border-radius to cards, SBOX blocks, accordions, or frames (0px only).
  • No multi-color rainbows: Never introduce blue, green, yellow, or violet accents—stick to Black, White, Greys, and Red.
  • No off-brand fonts: Never substitute Geist or Geist Mono with Inter, Roboto, serif, or script fonts.
  • No blur in static exports: Never apply backdrop-filter: blur() when generating PDFs or static posts.
