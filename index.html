03 / COMPOSITION, GEOMETRY & LEGIBILITY

Design Conventions

Master grid main: 1 stretch row + 1 stretch column with 136px inset (for 1080×1080 / 1920×1080 formats) and 1px gutter.

01 / PALETTE

Use black (#000000) and white (#FFFFFF) as the primary field. Choose one accent family per composition; do not mix all palette hues by default.

02 / HIERARCHY

Large, light, uppercase headlines. Sentence-case body copy. Monospaced labels (Geist Mono) for technical and navigational detail.

03 / COMPOSITION

Align to a clear edge; protect generous negative space. Keep ornamental borders and ticks at the outer perimeter, away from the message.

04 / GEOMETRY

Favor flat, angular marks and crisp rectangular framing. Maintain 0px border-radius across all containers and controls.

05 / LEGIBILITY

Use white (#FFFFFF) or a tested light tone (-L) for small text on dark fields. Check contrast at final size.

Strict Anti-Patterns (LLM Guardrails)

  • No rounded corners: Never apply border-radius to cards, buttons, or frames (0px only).
  • No multi-color rainbows: Never combine multiple accent families (e.g. Red + Blue + Green) in a single layout.
  • No off-brand fonts: Never substitute Geist or Geist Mono with Inter, Roboto, serif, or script fonts.
  • No shadows or blur: Avoid box-shadow, glassmorphism, and soft gradients.
