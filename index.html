01

Color System↘

Pure black (#000000) is the sole canvas background across all pages and components. White (#FFFFFF) is reserved strictly for h1–h3 headers and body text; everything else uses grey-l (#E8E5E0) or the active accent color.

02

Core Typography↘

Geist is used exclusively for h1, body text, and the lower CTA label. Geist Mono is used for everything else, set in uppercase. Body paragraphs are capped at 66ch for optimal reading comfort.

20ch Measure (12px — Too Narrow, Excessive Line Breaks)

We deliver change in companies by replacing vague corporate theory with structured action, clear ownership, and measurable execution.

66ch Measure (12px — Standard COM Reading Convention)

We deliver change in companies by replacing vague corporate theory with structured action, clear ownership, and measurable execution. Every paragraph stays within 66 characters so the eye tracks effortlessly across large monitors.

120ch Measure (12px — Too Wide, Eye Tracking Fatigue)

We deliver change in companies by replacing vague corporate theory with structured action, clear ownership, and measurable execution. When lines stretch to 120 characters across wide displays, readers lose their place returning to the left margin.
