01 Palette

Color System

Ordered sequence: Red (default), Blue, Green, Orange, Yellow, Teal, Violet, White + Grey, Black. Pure white is strictly reserved for h1–h3 headers and body text; all other text uses grey-l or the active accent color.

02 Typography

Core Typography

Geist is used exclusively for h1 and body text. Geist Mono is used for everything else, set in uppercase. Proportional vertical spacing to body text: 64px after h1, 40px after h2, and 24px after h3–h6.

Non-Web Border Stroke (Slide Decks & Print): 2px Inside Base Spacing Grid: 8px Increments
03 Box Primitives

Banners & Containers

Banners and box headers always hug their content width with 24px vertical and 40px horizontal padding. Box headers sit flush at the top of the container, while interior box content uses 64px vertical and 72px horizontal padding.

04 Governance

Design Rules

Functional minimalism enforced by strict token bindings, an 8px spacing grid, and clear typographic separation.

01 Color Scope

Pure white (#FFFFFF) is strictly reserved for h1–h3 headers and body text. Everything else uses grey-l (#E8E5E0) or the active accent color.

02 Font Roles

Geist is used exclusively for h1 and body text. Geist Mono is used for h2–h6, labels, banners, and buttons, always in uppercase.

03 Proportional Gaps

All spacing follows multiples of 8px. Minimum gap from headings to body text: 64px for h1, 40px for h2, and 24px for h3–h6.

04 Box Anatomy

Banners and box headers hug their text (padding: 24px 40px) at the top of the container. Interior box content uses padding: 64px 72px.

Strict Anti-Patterns

  • No rounded corners: 0px border-radius across all elements.
  • No off-role white text: never apply #FFFFFF to h4–h6, tags, or metadata.
  • No terminal cosplay: never use slashes or bracketed flavor text in UI labels.
  • No multi-color mixing: use Black + White/Grey + 1 Accent Family per composition.
